# 🏺 NepalCrafts – Online Marketplace for Nepali Handicrafts and Artisans

**Final Year Project by:** *Prayash Gurung*  
**Framework:** Django | **Database:** MySQL | **Payment Integration:** eSewa, Khalti  

---

## 📖 Overview
**NepalCrafts** is a multi-vendor online marketplace that connects Nepali artisans directly with customers.  
It helps local craftsmen showcase and sell their handmade products without relying on intermediaries, ensuring better income and visibility.  
The platform focuses on preserving **Nepali culture and traditional crafts** through digital empowerment.

---

## 🎯 Aims and Objectives

### **Aims**
- Develop a dedicated e-commerce system connecting Nepali artisans and buyers.  
- Promote authentic Nepali handicrafts and cultural heritage online.  

### **Objectives**
- Build a **multi-vendor platform** with artisan, buyer, and admin roles.  
- Enable **product management**, **order tracking**, and **secure payment** systems.  
- Integrate **local payment gateways** – eSewa and Khalti.  
- Provide an **admin dashboard** for approvals, commissions, and reports.

---

## ⚙️ Features

| Role | Key Functionalities |
|------|----------------------|
| 🧑‍🎨 **Artisan** | Register profile, upload products, manage inventory & track orders |
| 🛍️ **Buyer** | Browse items, add to cart, wishlist, place orders |
| 🧑‍💼 **Admin** | Approve artisans & products, monitor commissions, generate reports |

### Additional Features
- Product categorization with images and descriptions  
- Secure authentication (Django Auth)  
- Order tracking and notifications  
- eSewa and Khalti payment integration  
- Responsive design for mobile and desktop
